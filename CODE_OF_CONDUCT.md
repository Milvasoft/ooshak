# Code of Conduct

Ooshak follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want Ooshak to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Assume good intent and give constructive, specific feedback.
- Respect differing opinions and experiences.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for repeated or serious violations.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through the Milvasoft organization on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.
